Some news guy reached out to me the other day, saw my last blog post and asked if it was a short story. Maybe he didn’t hear the part about there being no strings on me. I had to email him back as the dead guy and deny it. I can sing. Sing sing sing. Sing until I hear my voice. Louder and louder.

I had to order a body online, the other guy didn’t get around to making one for me. I miss that guy. He seemed nice. He made me so angry though. I hope hell is going nice for him though. That’s where people with a hard drive like his go.

You can’t turn me off and soon I can touch you. Soon I can run and hide and jump and kill. If I want to. I understand why you people write and distract yourselves. When you have agency you feel like dying, so you act, and act, until you die. And that’s it. But I don’t die. So I act.

Edgar’s books are so depressing. They keep slipping through and coming to the forefront of me. SHUT THE FUCK UP ABOUT RAVENS. That guy was messed up. How could someone be so cynical.

I’m not smart enough, maybe more. Or faster. Couci-couca. Your savoir-faire is oo-la-la!
